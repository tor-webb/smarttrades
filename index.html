  • Activate: Navigate directly to the Promotions tab and select the 50% Deposit Bonus to opt in.
  • Deposit: Fund your account using your preferred payment method after the bonus has been successfully activated.

By participating in this promotion, you agree to adhere to the following operational guidelines:

Referrers earn 20% of the first confirmed deposit made by each user they introduce. Referral bonuses are credited to the referrer's Real account balance, are subject to the same trading volume requirement as the First Deposit Bonus, and cannot be earned on self-referrals or duplicate accounts.
